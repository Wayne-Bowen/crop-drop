About CropDrop

Food shouldn't need four middlemen.

CropDrop is a marketplace where smallholder farmers sell straight to the households, hotels, schools and exporters who buy what they grow.

Why we built it.

A farmer in Nakuru grows tomatoes. By the time they reach a kitchen in Nairobi they've passed through several hands and picked up several markups. The farmer got the least and the buyer paid the most.

So we made a place where the farmer lists the produce, sets the price and the buyer orders it. That's the entire idea. The rest is making it work properly.

Three roles

Who does what.

FarmersList produce, set prices and keep stock up to date.
BuyersSearch, order and follow the order to delivery.
AdminsApprove farmers and listings, and move orders along.
How we keep it honest

The boring rules that matter.

  • Every farmer account is approved by an administrator.
  • Every listing is checked before it appears on the market.
  • Prices at checkout always come from the farmer's listing.
  • Stock counts down as orders come in, so you can't buy what's gone.
