Questions

Fair enough. Ask away.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Browse, search and fill a cart without signing up. You only need a buyer account when you place the order.

How do farmers get approved?

An administrator checks each new farmer account and each listing before it goes live on the market.

Can I see where my order is?

Yes. Every order moves through placed, confirmed, dispatched and delivered. The current status shows on your My orders page.

What do I get after ordering?

A receipt with every item, farmer, quantity and price. Print it or download it as a PDF.
